# clients

The one list of Clients of an install, run by the space's Worker. A library with no Worker of its own. Sources PUSH Clients in; the space asks no one. What it is: `README.md`. Terms: `CONTEXT.md`. The decision: the space's `docs/adr/0021`.

## Working here

- **The layers are the knowledge app's and the calendar's:** `schema.ts` (migrations as code, appended and never edited; `test/schema.test.ts` pins the count), `store.ts` (every rule, over a SQL port, no runtime), `space.ts`, `inbox.ts` and `source.ts` (what each face does, with its store passed in), `durable.ts` (the only file that touches `cloudflare:workers`), `store-access.ts` (the object's name and the typed way to reach it, with no runtime import, so the space and the tests load it).
- **The contract is in the workspace package's `src/clients.ts`, not here.** A method added to a face is added there first (the workspace package's `test/space-contract.test.ts` pins the lists; `test/faces.test.ts` pins each face to its list; `test/durable.test.ts` pins that the object has `call` and the operator's three and nothing else public, because the runtime exposes every method of a Durable Object class over RPC, `private` or not).
- **Nothing here may know about a source.** No `fetch`, no binding to a site, no field named for Stripe or for a company site's sign-in address: a source says what it says in `source.extra`, which the part keeps and never reads. The space package's `test/wrangler.test.ts` greps this package's `src/` for the words, and the space's, the inbox's and the company layer's `src/` for a source's field names (how a source's extra fields are labelled, and the one key the inbox suggests by, are the declaration's `clientsDoor.fields` and `suggestByAddressKey`). A source is a sender behind the Clients door (the space package's `src/clients-door.ts`); the only source-specific files are the declaration's `clientsDoor` and the Access application for its token.
- **Nothing deletes a Client,** and nothing a source says archives one. Archived is the space's alone.
- **The owners and the one source are the company declaration's** (the install's `workspace.json`, bundled into the space's Worker through `@clients/declaration`): `owners` make, edit, archive and put back; the `clientsDoor.source` name is the only name a push is accepted in, by the actor `source:<name>`.
- **Secrets and vars:** none in this package. The Clients door's audience and token id are the space's vars (`CF_ACCESS_AUD_CLIENTS`, `CLIENTS_ACCESS_CLIENT_ID`).

## Checks

In a worktree, after `bun install` at the root, from the clients package: `bun test`, `bun run typecheck`. From the workspace, space and infra packages: `bun test` and `bun run typecheck` (the space's full run is slow; run `test/clients-door.test.ts`, `wrangler.test.ts` and `worker-exports.test.ts` for this package). A worktree has no Cloudflare access.

## Where it breaks

- **Its identity is (the SPACE Worker, class `ClientsDO`) and the object's name** (`CLIENTS_NAME` in `src/store-access.ts`). Changing any is a new, empty list beside the stored one, and unlike the calendar's, nothing can be read again to fill it: a Client made in the space is only here. The store keeps Cloudflare's 30 days of history; `bookmark` (the operator's) is the marker to note before an apply that migrates it.
- **Migrations are appended, never edited or reordered** (`src/schema.ts`). A store a newer build migrated refuses an older one, so undoing a deploy that migrated is a forward fix, not a redeploy.
- **Never keep a stub of another object.** A stub whose target was reset or aborted (the owners' restart routes use `ctx.abort`) is broken for good, and an object holding one fails every call until it restarts itself (this happened once in production). `storeFor` and `objectFor` in `src/store-access.ts` get a fresh stub per call; `test/store-access.test.ts` holds it. This object holds no stub of any other object, and its readers (the inbox: the space package's `src/inbox-clients.ts`) must be handed `storeFor`'s face, not a stub.
- **A number is the row's own id.** It is what every link, address and signing record will name, so it is never reused and never changed. A source's `preferredNumber` is honoured only when free; a Client made in the space is numbered from `CLIENT_LOCAL_NUMBER_FROM` while the install has a source, and the source's own numbers below that stay its own.
- **The names follow a source only while they equal what it last said** (`said` is kept whole; the Client's own is cut to `CLIENT_NAME_MAX` and `CLIENT_LEGAL_NAME_MAX`). Changing `followName` or `followLegal` changes which Clients count as edited in the space.
- **The live part `clients` is told after every stored change and no other time** (`ClientsStore.changeCount`), so a push that is already there tells no one. Settings › Clients, People's picker, the Inbox menu and the people pills read again on it (`useFresh`/`useLive` in the space package's `src/client`; `test/live-screens.test.tsx` holds the list).
- **The screens are the space's** (the space package's `src/clients-settings.ts` puts the part's Client and the inbox's people under it together; `client/ClientsView.tsx` draws them). Whether a name still follows its source is the store's rule (`followName`, `followLegal`), computed in the Worker and sent to the page; the page never restates it.
