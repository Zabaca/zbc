# The inbox runs in the space's Worker, and its store is handed to it

Status: accepted: one app at the space's address, each store moved by Cloudflare's transfer. This is the third stage of the move; it is amended at the end by the stage that followed.
**Supersedes [0001](0001-one-front-door-four-tabs-the-apps-keep-their-data-and-outsiders-their-addresses.md)'s "the apps keep their data" for the inbox, the last app it held to** (forms, the knowledge app and meet were taken out of it by [0015](0015-forms-and-the-knowledge-app-run-in-the-spaces-worker-and-the-knowledge-store-is-handed-to-it.md) and [0016](0016-meets-chat-calls-and-phone-run-in-the-spaces-worker-and-its-two-stores-are-handed-to-it.md)); amends [0005](0005-the-inbox-s-other-views-come-in-the-sender-serves-both-kinds-of-device-and-the-old-address-moves-people-only-when-told.md), [0011](0011-writing-from-the-space-a-new-email-is-sends-third-shape-with-a-mailbox-chosen-and-a-draft-edit-and-a-spam-call-are-the-inboxs-own.md) and [0014](0014-conversations-is-split-back-into-inbox-and-meet-and-the-old-addresses-redirect.md) in one respect: the inbox's answer is a call in this Worker, not a service binding.
The sign-in rule is the company layer's [ADR 0003](../../../workspace/docs/adr/0003-the-space-checks-the-sign-in-once-and-the-parts-in-its-worker-are-handed-the-person.md), which this extends to the inbox, and [0002](../../../workspace/docs/adr/0002-one-service-token-comes-through-the-space-on-one-path-to-one-app.md) (Claude's door), which holds with the inbox now in-process.
Follows 0015 and 0016, whose shape (safe defaults, step files, a guard on every step, a separate cleanup commit) this repeats.

## What changed

- **The inbox's team-facing part runs in the Worker**: `createInboxPartFactory` (`packages/inbox/src/space-part.ts`) makes one value, called with the person the one sign-in check named (`SignedIn`). Its methods are `InboxForSpace` (the contract, now taking `SignedIn` where it took the Access assertion) and `claude`, which hands a request on Claude's door to the inbox's own router.
  The inbox keeps its one rule: `ownerGate` admits the owner and nobody else, a teammate included, in the words the space has always shown.
- **Claude's door (`/agent/inbox`) is checked once, in the space** (`packages/space/src/agent.ts`: the audience of the agent application, a service token only, as before) and the request goes on **without the assertion, the cookie or the token's own two headers**, to the inbox's router, which is handed the token that check verified (`Deps.named`, a `WeakMap` keyed by the request, as forms' operator is) and applies its own rule: only Claude's token, by its id (`INBOX_ACCESS_CLIENT_ID`), is the agent; `/api/` is all that is served. The router still verifies the assertion itself where it is built without `named` (the gap's `ClaudeInbox`, and its own tests).
- **The inbox's store, `InboxDO`, is handed to the space's Worker by Cloudflare's transfer** (one class). Nothing is copied. It is found by the same fixed name (`INBOX_NAME`, `packages/inbox/src/store-access.ts`). The class is `packages/inbox/src/durable.ts`, the file both Workers build it from; **the space defines it as a subclass** (`packages/space/src/inbox-do.ts`) that overrides one method, the knowledge port, because the object runs with this Worker's env and in this Worker `KNOWLEDGE` is the knowledge store's own class, not the service binding it is in the inbox's.
- **A moved object runs with the receiver's env.** The space therefore carries every binding the object reads, before the hand-over: the raw-mail bucket `RAW`, the sender `EMAIL` (now the install's forms and meet addresses and one address per shared business mailbox), Workers AI `AI`, the Client directory `CLIENTS`, the declared mailboxes `INBOX_MAILBOXES`, Claude's token id, the Google client id and Gmail's topic, and the secrets `INBOX_GOOGLE_CLIENT_SECRET`, `INBOX_VAPID_KEY` and `ANTHROPIC_API_KEY`.
- **The operator's three**, owner-only, on the space (`POST /api/inbox/store/{bookmark,counts,restart}`): Cloudflare's bookmark of the object's history, a count of rows by table (numbers under names, `packages/inbox/src/counts.ts`) and an explicit restart. The inbox's own restore (`prepareRestore`, `confirmRestore`) is what goes back to a bookmark, so there is no restore route.

**What did not move at this stage**: Gmail's push endpoint, Cloudflare Email Routing's `email()` (the zone instances name the Worker `inbox`), the delivery-report queue's consumer and the daily pass (the cron) stayed on the inbox Worker, each a call on the moved store through its binding `INBOX` (`script_name: space`), until the entrances moved (see the amendment at the end). The cron is in exactly one Worker at every step: the inbox's.

## What each Worker was at this stage

| Worker | Is | Until |
|---|---|---|
| `space` | the page, the one sign-in check (and Claude's door's), forms' operator API and reads, the knowledge base and its store, meet's chat, calls, phone and live socket and its two stores, **the inbox's Inbox tab, People's mail reads, Settings, Claude's door and its store** | it is the whole app (the end state) |

For the gap of the move also `SpaceInbox` and `ClaudeInbox` | the entrances move; the cleanup deletes the two entrypoints; the cutover deletes the Worker |

The inbox Worker is the exception to "no compatibility shims" the move itself plans (a deployed Worker, not a forwarding module, needed because Google and Cloudflare name it); nothing may be added to it.

## Reading an older note

Until the cleanup that followed the move of this stage, the inbox Worker also exported `SpaceInbox` and `ClaudeInbox`, the old named entrypoints the LIVE space bound, which verified the Access assertion the space forwarded against `SPACE_ACCESS_AUD` and `AGENT_ACCESS_AUD` and then called the very methods this Worker now calls in-process (`packages/inbox/src/space-entry.ts`), so that the old space kept its Inbox tab and Claude's door through the steps that came before the new space was deployed and could be put back until then. The new space binds neither.
**A note in a README, ADR, runbook or code comment that says the space binds `INBOX` or `INBOX_AGENT`, forwards the assertion to the inbox, or that the inbox holds `SPACE_ACCESS_AUD` or `AGENT_ACCESS_AUD`, was true until then and is not now.** Those notes are left as the record of what was built and deployed at the time; this section is the one place that says so.

## Consequences

- **Every change to the inbox reaches two Workers, and only by an apply of each** (both build the class; the space runs the part; the inbox Worker runs the entrances). Neither is in CI. Apply `inbox`, then `space`.
- **A bad deploy of the space now takes mail, chat, calls and Claude's drafting down with the page**, as with every change that ships in the one Worker; the machine entrances keep answering on the inbox Worker as long as the space's class exists (a push, a message or a report that finds no store is retried or bounced; the raw copy is written first).
- **The knowledge reads of the assistant are in-process**, so the knowledge forwarder is no longer on the inbox's path (the cutover deletes it).
- **Counts are compared in three groups**, because the inbox is never quiet (`packages/inbox/src/counts.ts`): EQUAL for what only the owner's acts change; ONLY UP for what an arrival writes; reported for owed work and statuses.
- **The way back is zbc applies** (the rehearsed reverse, three deploys), never `wrangler rollback`. A plain apply during the move is guarded by the live `INBOX` binding of both Workers (`classHome`, kept in the infra module); the defaults are the safe state before the move and the cleanup makes the end state the default. Two live states share identical bindings (after step 4 and after `back2`): never take `back3` before `back2`, and after step 4 alone the way back finishes step 5 first.
- **A moved object that is awake through the hand-over keeps running the giving Worker's code and env until it restarts; one that is asleep wakes on the receiver's.** The store's object has an explicit restart, a bookmark and a counts read, reachable only by the owner through the space; they touch no message, draft or contact. The restart's proof is the line `inbox: the store object started` in the **space's** tail.
- **What only a live run shows**, and what to rehearse first on a throwaway pair: the giver's `email()`, queue consumer and cron forwarding to a moved class, an alarm set before the hand-over, the receiver's env seen from a moved object, and the subclass.
- **A move's own record** (the counts before and after, the bookmarks, any deviation) is written by whoever runs it, with the install; this ADR is not where it goes.

## Amended by the stage that followed

The machine entrances did move, and the `inbox` Worker is deleted: Gmail's push endpoint is on a second Custom Domain of the space's Worker (chosen by hostname first in every request, `src/worker.ts`; no Access application on it), `email()` and the delivery queue's consumer and the daily pass are the space's, the routing rules name `space`, and `packages/inbox` has no Worker, wrangler file, instance or build of its own. The "exception to no compatibility shims" above therefore ended with that stage, and "every change to the inbox reaches two Workers" is no longer true: it reaches one. The decision about what runs where, and the rest, stand.
