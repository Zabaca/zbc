# Channel admin is meet's answer, drawn in the conversation, and the invitation is the one email it causes

Status: accepted, the first step of retiring meet's `/start`. Meet's side is [packages/meet ADR 0007](../../../meet/docs/adr/0007-channel-admin-in-the-space-the-same-rules-asked-as-the-same-teammate-one-email-once-per-key.md); the contract is `packages/workspace/src/meet-for-space.ts` ("Channel admin").

## What the space does about channels

**New channel, Invite a guest and Archive channel, and what goes with them, are the space's.** Conversations gets two buttons above the rows, **New channel** (a name, and Private or Open, in `/start`'s words) and **Browse channels** (the open channels not joined, with Join); and a channel's conversation gets its people beside it, where the profile is for a person: who is in it with a tag (the company's name for a teammate, Guest, Invited), what the person may do with each (Message, Take out, Remove from workspace, Delete everything), Add someone you can see, Invite a guest, and Rename, Leave and Archive. The screens are `src/client/Channels.tsx`; the Worker's side, `src/channels.ts`, is one call to meet each.

**The space decides nothing about who may do what.** Meet answers `channel` with a `may` for the channel and for each person in it, from its own rules (the maker and the owner archive; the owner removes any guest and a teammate the guests they invited; the owner deletes a person), and the page draws a button only where it says so. A refusal meet still makes (a name taken, a teammate's address as a guest, the hour's emails used) arrives in meet's words and is shown as they are. Archive, Remove from workspace and Delete everything ask in the page first, in `/start`'s words; Take out, Leave, Rename, Add and Join act at once, as at `/start`.

**A channel made in the space is in the list at once.** The list drew no row for a channel nobody had written in; a channel made here would have vanished until its first message. Meet now says when each channel was made, the row is at that time with "No messages yet.", and opening it works. A meet that says no time (an older one) keeps the old behaviour. Direct messages are unchanged: one with nothing in it still has no row.

**The writes go through the one POST guard, and meet is asked once.** `/api/meet/channel-create`, `channel-invite` and `channel-change` are POSTs on their own paths, accepted only with this host's Origin and a JSON body (the guard every other write goes through), and each is one call to meet with the request's assertion, never retried: one the space loses track of is "outcome unknown" and the page offers no second try. Making a channel and inviting a guest carry a compose key, which meet holds, so the same key twice is one channel and **one email**. The invitation's key is the filled-in form's, as a reply's is the message's (`compose.ts`): one key kept through a refusal (meet releases it) and through an outcome the page could not read (meet may hold it, and pressing again is then answered as the first), a new one only after the invitation went through, and the boxes cleared only then (they stay when the email did not leave, so Invite again is one press).

**The invitation is meet's email, sent when a teammate presses Invite.** The space sends nothing: meet sends the guest's invitation, from the install's meet sender address, linking to the guest's own door at `/w`, when Invite is pressed, once per key; typing in the boxes, making a channel, joining, adding, taking a guest out and archiving send nothing. The page says so beside the button, says "Invited <name>. They get an email from <the sender address>." when it left, and, when it did not, says the guest is in the channel and to press Invite again. The guest's door is the guest path `/w` of the space's host (ADR 0018): the page links a guest to nothing, and a guest never reaches the rest of the space (their address is not on the Teammate list).

## What was not decided here

Changing a channel between Private and Open after it is made: `/start` could not either. The things `/start` still has that are not channel admin (a first direct message with someone, editing and deleting a message, a person's settings, the Phone and past calls' parts already in the space) are listed in the README's "After channel admin moves in".
