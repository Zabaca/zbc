# space: Context

The front door at `space.<company domain>` (for the invented install, `space.northfield.example`), the one address a company's team works at.
A Worker that checks the sign-in ONCE on every request, serves the seven-tab page, answers `/api/me`, draws the Forms tab's screens itself and answers their API calls and logos with forms' own app, which RUNS IN IT,
runs the knowledge app and meet's chat, calls and Phone in it too, and draws the Inbox (email) from the inbox, which runs in it too. It is the one app
([ADR 0015](docs/adr/0015-forms-and-the-knowledge-app-run-in-the-spaces-worker-and-the-knowledge-store-is-handed-to-it.md)):
it OWNS the knowledge base's Durable Object, meet's two stores ([ADR 0016](docs/adr/0016-meets-chat-calls-and-phone-run-in-the-spaces-worker-and-its-two-stores-are-handed-to-it.md)) and the inbox's mail store ([ADR 0017](docs/adr/0017-the-inbox-runs-in-the-spaces-worker-and-its-store-is-handed-to-it.md)), and binds forms' database and bucket, so every app's data is this Worker's or bound by it. It is the only Worker of the four apps and `space.<company domain>` the only address people use: meet and forms have no Worker or host of their own. People is the directory of the people those three know,
with a profile for each ([ADR 0009](docs/adr/0009-people-is-three-reads-merged-by-address-and-the-profile-is-the-inboxs-contact-with-three-fields.md)). Today is seven reads of the three apps, cut on the company's day, each row opening where the work is
([ADR 0006](docs/adr/0006-today-is-seven-reads-cut-on-the-companys-day-each-row-opens-where-the-work-is.md)). Conversations was one list of people and channels,
a merge of two reads ([ADR 0003](docs/adr/0003-conversations-is-a-merge-of-two-reads-one-row-per-person-joined-by-address.md)); a row opens that
person's or channel's conversation with the reply, the draft waiting and the profile beside it, and the
three POST paths that send and mark read ([ADR 0004](docs/adr/0004-a-conversation-is-one-history-per-person-and-every-send-carries-a-key-made-once.md)). It was then split back into two
tabs, **Inbox** (email alone) and **Meet** (chat, calls and the Phone), each drawn from one app's read by `scope`, with every `/conversations` address redirecting
([ADR 0014](docs/adr/0014-conversations-is-split-back-into-inbox-and-meet-and-the-old-addresses-redirect.md)).

Not meet's "workspace" (channels and direct messages in `packages/meet`), and not `packages/workspace` (the company layer, a
library this Worker imports).

## Glossary

- **Space**: the one address, `space.<company domain>`, and everything on it.
- **Tab**: one of Today, Inbox, Meet, Calendar, Forms, People, Knowledge, in that order, of which an install has the ones its switches give it (no Calendar without a Google account, no Inbox without a mailbox of any kind; the company layer's ADR 0004). Their names, order and
  paths are the company layer's (`TABS` in `packages/workspace/src/install.ts`), not this package's. The space opens on
  Today.
- **Row**: one person or one channel in the Inbox's or Meet's list (a **scope**: `mail` is the Inbox, which asks the inbox alone, `chat` is Meet, which asks meet alone), at the time of its newest item; the contract is
  `packages/workspace/src/rows.ts`. **Part**: one source of rows, mail (the inbox) or chat (meet); a part that does not answer is
  named in a line and the other's rows stay. **Unread** on a row is one number in two units (mail counts conversations, chat counts messages).
- **Compose key**: made once by the page for each message being written and sent with every send; the apps hold it, so nothing goes out twice. A send
  the space loses track of is **outcome unknown**: the page says where to check and never offers it again.
- **People**: a tab, the directory of everyone the space knows, grouped **Clients** (a contact linked to a Client the site lists), **Outside** (other contacts the inbox judges a person, and channel guests) and **Team**
  (the company layer's people, and meet's teammates). An **entry** is one person joined across the inbox, meet and the team by lowercase address; its **key** is its first address (else `mail:<contactId>`, else
  `chat:<personId>`) and its **conversation key** is the list's row key (the same in the Inbox and in Meet), or null while nothing has been said with them; `said` tells which side has something (email, chat or both), and the page has an Open email and an Open chat for them. A person's **profile** is their inbox contact plus three fields People adds, **business, role and
  notes**, kept on the contact with the **revision** a write must name (the inbox's migration 12); a teammate's is the company layer's person. **Automated senders** (newsletters, receipts, no-reply addresses) are counted and not
  listed. A **merge** of two entries that are one person is undone from the line that follows it (`undoContactMerge`), which puts the other person back whole.
- **The menu (tree)**: the inbox's own left menu in the space: under Inbox in the rail on a computer, a sheet from a button above the list on a
  phone. Meet has its own, the same way: All, Chat, Channels, Calls and (where the person has a Phone) Phone. Views (Inbox, Unread, Drafts, Bulk, Closed), Mailboxes, Clients (no Contacts item: People is its own tab), and Settings at the foot with Gmail's and each mailbox's status lines. Picking
  one narrows the list through the address (`?view=`). Its counts are of rows, the list's own unit (a person), counted by the inbox over every conversation (`packages/inbox/src/store/row-counts.ts`, held to the lists by `test/nav-counts.test.ts`) so a number is how many rows the view shows, however far back they go; a Drafts count adds the drafts on no conversation, which the list shows above its rows. A **Client pill** on a row is the
  Client its person is linked to.
- **Settings**: the menu behind the gear (the foot of the menu): Notifications, Claude's sending, Mailboxes and spam check with the Gmail connection, Clients, Restore. Everything on
  it is the owner's, decided by the inbox ([ADR 0005](docs/adr/0005-the-inbox-s-other-views-come-in-the-sender-serves-both-kinds-of-device-and-the-old-address-moves-people-only-when-told.md)).
- **Calendar**: the tab after Meet: the calendars of the Google accounts the install connects, merged by time into an agenda by day, coloured by account, with the meetings booked in Meet among them, and an event's card (an address, `/calendar/event/<key>`). Read only. The calendar app (`packages/calendar`) keeps its own copy of Google's calendars in a store of its own, synced by Google's push; it holds no Google token (the grant is the inbox's: one permission per account serves mail and calendar). Today's Later today and Tomorrow list its events beside Meet's meetings; Settings, Calendars lists each account's calendars with a switch for whether each is shown. Why, and what Google's documents say: [ADR 0020](docs/adr/0020-the-calendar-is-the-seventh-tab-over-a-store-of-its-own-synced-from-google-by-push-and-it-reads-through-the-inboxs-one-grant.md).
- **Knowledge**: the last tab, the organisation's one knowledge base (the knowledge app's, `packages/knowledge`), read and changed by the owner alone, with a row of choices at the top (All, then each
  business of the company declaration; the choice is in the address, `?business=`) that shows exactly what that business's assistant reads. Each entry names the **businesses** it is for; a change of
  businesses is a version, kept in History. Claude's proposals wait under "Waiting for you"; one written against an entry that has since moved on is **out of date** and can only be rejected. The only
  place knowledge is changed: the Inbox tree and Settings have no Knowledge item. Why: [ADR 0008](docs/adr/0008-knowledge-is-the-fifth-tab-and-the-only-place-knowledge-changes.md).
- **Huddle, call, meeting**: a **huddle** is a call with teammates started in a direct message or channel by anyone in it; a **call** is one with outside people by link (a **call link** put in an email, or a **New call** with a row of its own); a **meeting** is a call booked with a time from a conversation. Each is a **card** in the conversation it was made in (who is in it now, "Call ended, 12 min", Join, Copy link): a huddle's in its direct message or channel in Meet, and a call link's or meeting's in the email conversation in the Inbox it was made from, which therefore asks meet for its person's cards and nothing else of chat. They are listed under Meet's **Calls** view with their saved chat. Meet makes every **link**, on its public address; the space copies it. An **invitation** is text in the reply box, sent only when a person presses Send. [ADR 0009](docs/adr/0009-calls-are-cards-in-their-conversation-and-the-phone-is-meets-screen-held-by-the-shell.md).
- **Channel admin**: New channel, Browse channels (with Join), and a channel's people (Invite a guest, Add someone, Take out, Remove from workspace, Delete everything, Rename, Leave, Archive), the parts of meet's `/start` that moved here before `/start` was retired. Every rule is meet's, asked as the same teammate, and its `may` says which button to draw; the space decides nothing about who may. A channel with nothing said in it has a row from the moment it is made (`MeetChannel.createdAt`). **The invitation** is the one email meet sends for the space: when a teammate presses Invite, once per compose key, from the install's meet sender address, linking to the guest's own door at `space.<company domain>/w/`. [ADR 0012](docs/adr/0012-channel-admin-is-meets-answer-drawn-in-the-conversation-and-the-invitation-is-the-one-email-it-causes.md).
- **Phone**: the Phone conversation's row (voicemails, missed calls, Call back) and meet's own dial pad drawn inside the space, held by the shell so a call rings anywhere; not set up until the install has a Telnyx account. Its line settings and each person's 911 address are in Settings.
- **Guest paths**: the one exception to "nothing on the host is reachable without the sign-in" ([ADR 0018](docs/adr/0018-guests-and-signers-use-three-open-paths-of-the-spaces-host-with-no-sign-in-and-a-policy-that-keeps-their-pages-to-those-paths.md)):
  `/r/` (a call: its lobby, the call and its API), `/w/` (a channel's guests: the emailed-code sign-in and their channels) and `/s/` (a signer's pages and API, and a client's logo). Each is answered before the person check,
  with the credential its path takes and nothing else, behind its own Access application with a bypass, and its page is a bundle of its own with a strict content policy. Everything else on the host needs the sign-in.
  Meet and forms have no host of their own where outsiders could land; nothing is redirected from an app's former address.
- **Forms sections**: Clients, Envelopes, Forms, Documents, Seal, History: the screens of the Forms tab, at `/forms/<section>`, a tree under the tab in the rail and a row across the top on a phone.
- **Document card**: a document sent from a conversation, drawn in its history ([ADR 0010](docs/adr/0010-a-document-sent-from-a-conversation-carries-one-ref-and-is-found-by-any-and-the-card-is-forms-read-drawn-by-time.md)): title, state ("Out for signature, 1 of 2 signed", "Signed and back"),
  when it last moved and a button that opens it in Forms. The same card says the signed copy is back when the document completes. It has no field for a name, an address, a link or an answer, so a document holding a private answer shows its title and state like any other.
  **Send for signature** is the header's link to Forms' Send with the conversation's person filled in. **Conversation ref**: a conversation's id in an app (`{ app: 'inbox' | 'meet', id }`, `ConversationRef` in the company layer): a person's conversation has several refs (a mail contact per address, a direct message),
  a document carries one and is found by any; the row's key is not one, because it changes.
- **New email**: an email written from scratch in the Inbox tab ([ADR 0011](docs/adr/0011-writing-from-the-space-a-new-email-is-sends-third-shape-with-a-mailbox-chosen-and-a-draft-edit-and-a-spam-call-are-the-inboxs-own.md)): to any address, from a mailbox the person chooses (Gmail, or a business mailbox by id; never an address typed), with files only from a business mailbox. `send`'s third shape, so it has the compose key and every refusal a reply has. **Edit** on a draft's card is the inbox's own draft edit at the revision shown; **Mark as spam** and **Move to the Inbox** over an email thread are the inbox's own spam call.
- **A draft on no conversation**: a draft with no conversation yet (a first email to a new address, a first LinkedIn message to a new contact). It is listed under Inbox, Drafts, "Not on a conversation yet", and opened at `/inbox/draft/<draft id>`, where its card has Edit, Send as it stands (an email) and Copy address, Copy words and Mark sent (a text or LinkedIn message), by the same rules as a draft in a conversation. The address keeps working: once the draft is sent or marked sent, or is on a conversation, it opens that person's conversation.
- **New message**: the first message to someone the person can see but has no conversation with ([ADR 0013](docs/adr/0013-messages-and-settings-in-the-space-chat-is-meets-answer-drawn-where-the-conversation-is.md)). Not a write of its own: it is `post` by `personId`, which opens the direct message as it posts, once per compose key. **New line**: where unread began when a conversation was opened, kept for as long as it stays open (`newFrom`). **Chat settings**: the name chat messages carry, the 15-minute nudge and the privacy note, meet's and changed through it (Settings, Chat).
- **Origin**: `/api/me` answers `origin`, the space's own address (`https://space.<company domain>/`), which every link the apps make is on (a call link is `<origin>r/<name>`). There is no address of any app to link to: the four apps
  are tabs, and there is no bar of links beside them.

## What to know before changing it

- **The sign-in check runs on every request**, the page and its files included (`run_worker_first: true` in
  the Worker's settings, which the install kit makes), and reads the `Cf-Access-Jwt-Assertion` header only. Never serve an asset before the check, and never read
  the cookie: a cookie-only request once got past the Access edge.
- **Deploys by hand**, not in CI: an apply restarts the running app, so the operator picks the moment. A change to
  the install's declaration (a person added or removed) needs
  `bunx @zabaca/zbc apply <env> space` (which applies `space-access` first). Removing is the case that fails open: the person stays in the space's Access policy and its bundled list until the space is applied.
- **Four Access applications on the host**: the space's, on the whole host (a month, `space-access`), the Forms application, on
  `/forms` (a day, `space-forms-access`), Claude's door, on `/agent/inbox` (no person, one service token, no cookie, `space-agent-access`), and the Telegram bridge's door, on `/agent/telegram` (no person, a token of its own, no cookie, `space-telegram-access`). The Worker checks the audience the path calls for
  (`audienceFor` in `src/app.ts` for the first two; `src/agent.ts` and `src/telegram.ts` answer the others before the person check), and the
  Forms tab forwards forms' operator API and logos to forms (`src/forms.ts`). Why, and what is not settled:
  [ADR 0002](docs/adr/0002-forms-is-a-frame-on-its-own-admin-with-a-day-long-sign-in-of-its-own.md) for Forms; for the door,
  [the company layer's ADR 0002](../workspace/docs/adr/0002-one-service-token-comes-through-the-space-on-one-path-to-one-app.md): the one place a service token is let in, never as a person.
- **The Forms tab's screens are this package's, in Tailwind** (`src/client/forms/`, [ADR 0007](docs/adr/0007-forms-screens-are-the-space-s-own-page-and-forms-keeps-the-api.md)): forms' admin moved in, with no frame. They
  import some of forms' own files by path (`@space/forms/src/...`; the list is in ADR 0007, "What is imported from forms"), so a change to one of those in forms needs the space
  applied too. A screen moves with `go()` and links with `href()` (`forms/route.ts`), never by writing the address; a call that finds the Forms sign-in gone reloads the page (`forms/session.ts`). The tests that show the screens are `e2e/`, over forms' real Worker.
- **Forms, the knowledge app and meet are parts of this Worker** (`src/parts.ts`): called as functions with the person the one sign-in check named, over a store this Worker owns or binds. There is no binding to any of them and no
  second check ([the company layer's ADR 0003](../workspace/docs/adr/0003-the-space-checks-the-sign-in-once-and-the-parts-in-its-worker-are-handed-the-person.md)). The Worker exports three Durable Object classes, `KnowledgeDO`, `RoomDO` and `WorkspaceDO`, handed over by Cloudflare's transfer.
  Meet's part (`packages/meet/src/space-part.ts`) also carries the dial pad: `/api/phone` is handed to its `fetch` for the person the check named, as the same request without its cookie. A teammate's page has ONE live socket, `GET /api/live`, handed to the company's live object (`LiveDO`, [ADR 0019](docs/adr/0019-one-live-object-per-company-holds-every-open-page-and-every-store-tells-it-after-a-change-is-stored.md)); the Worker exports four store classes and that one. The call API, the guest sign-in and Telnyx's webhook are this Worker's too, on the guest paths (`src/guests.ts`).
- **The inbox is a part that runs in this Worker (it used to be a service binding to a named entrypoint)** (`packages/inbox/src/space-part.ts`): it is handed the person the one sign-in check named (`SignedIn`) and applies its own rule (the owner only), over the store this Worker owns (`INBOX`, class `InboxDO`).
  Claude's door (`/agent/inbox`) is checked here once and the request is handed to the inbox's router in-process. The old service bindings `INBOX` and `INBOX_AGENT` (to `SpaceInbox` and `ClaudeInbox`) are gone. A read
  waits `ANSWER_WITHIN_MS` and then shows the other part. **Apply the space** when the inbox's code changes: it builds the class and also runs the machine entrances (Gmail's push endpoint, Cloudflare's mail, the delivery reports, the daily pass; `src/worker.ts`); there is no `inbox` Worker.
  Nothing the page reads is cached, and nothing the Worker reads from the inbox is kept.
- A change to what the apps answer (a field in a row) starts in `packages/workspace/src/rows.ts` and the two `*-for-space.ts` files, not here;
  what this package adds to a row (`ListedPerson`) is only what the page needs and the contract has no field for (`lastBy`, `sharedWith`, `mailbox`, `clients`, `drafts`, and `telegramChat`: a Telegram group or channel is a row under its own contact's name, and People leaves that contact out).
- The look is a prototype's: tabs down the side on a computer, a strip across the top with the icon above the label on a phone.
