# Calls in the space: a call is a Room with a record of what it is for, and every link is meet's own

Status: accepted. Builds on [0003](0003-workspace-one-object-two-doors-live-sockets-emailed-codes.md) (the Workspace object and its two doors), [0004](0004-phone-telnyx-behind-one-client-calls-held-until-meet-approves-rules-in-the-workspace-object.md) and [0005](0005-phone-calls-in-call-control-commands-ring-every-phone-then-the-cell-then-voicemail-in-the-phone-conversation.md) (the phone), and the company layer's contract (`packages/workspace/src/meet-for-space.ts`). The space's side is [packages/space ADR 0009](../../../space/docs/adr/0009-calls-are-cards-in-their-conversation-and-the-phone-is-meets-screen-held-by-the-shell.md).

## What was decided

**A call stays a Room.** Nothing about how a call is made, joined or metered changes: a Durable Object per call, the ledger instance's index of every call, the meter. What is new is a record in the Workspace object of **what a call is for** (`space_calls`): a **huddle** (the direct message or channel it was started in), a **call link** (the email conversation it was made from, by the space's row key), a **New call** (nothing), or a **meeting** (a time, the people invited, and its conversation or home). The Room and the ledger do not know any of that, and a call made at `/start` before this has no record and is read as the owner's (the ledger's maker decides).

**Who may start a huddle: anyone in the conversation.** `callBegin` asks the same questions `send` does (the person is in it, it is not the Phone conversation, a direct message still has its other person, the rate limit has room) for any actor, so a channel guest starts one at meet's own door (`POST /api/w/conversations/<id>/huddle`, the same handler the teammates' door runs) and the space, which only teammates reach, starts one through `SpaceMeet.startHuddle`. Every other kind of call is a teammate's (a guest has no way to make one: the core refuses). A huddle **posts one message** in its conversation (`Started a huddle: <link>`, with the call's name in the message's `extra`), so the conversation's newest, its unread and the live feed work as for any message, and meet's own page, which draws no card, still shows "Join call".

**Making a call is two core steps around the one thing the core cannot do.** `callBegin` (may they, and has this compose key made a call already) then, in the router, the meter and the Room and the ledger's index (`calls.ts`), then `callRecord` (the record and the key, one step; a huddle's message too). A fault between the last two leaves a call nobody is told about, which is harmless and is in the ledger. A write carries a compose key held a day, as `post` does: a refused first use releases it, a made call holds it, and a repeat answers the call the first use made.

**The meter refuses new calls as it refuses new joins.** From 800 GB of the account's free 1,000 GB `makeCall` is refused (`limit`, "Calls are paused until <first of next month>."), for a huddle, a call link, a New call and a meeting alike; a booking is refused too, because its Room is made at once. A person already in a call carries on, and a join to a call made before the trip is refused (`room.join`'s rule, unchanged). `meterOf` is the one reading of the ledger, used by `/start`'s usage and the space's.

**EVERY LINK IS MADE BY MEET, from `APP_ORIGIN`.** *(Amended by workspace ADR 0005: the address is the install's space host, `appOriginOf` in `src/wiring.ts`, and `APP_ORIGIN` is no longer a setting of the Worker.)* An answer that names a call carries its `link`; the space builds none and the page copies and opens exactly that, so a link copied inside the space opens on meet's own host, which needs no sign-in. `SpaceMeetDeps.appOrigin` is the Worker's variable and nothing the request carried. The lobby's `?as=<name>` fills the name box only (Join on a card carries the person's own name; Copy link does not), and is cleaned to the lobby's 40 characters.

**A meeting is booked, not sent.** `bookMeeting` records a time (between five minutes ago and a year ahead), a title, and who is invited (by address; in a direct message or channel, its people), makes the call now so its link works from then on, and sends nothing: meet sends no email here. The invitation is text the space puts in the email being written, which a person sends with the inbox's own send (and Claude never sends it: the inbox's rule is that only the owner's word sends). Who sees a meeting: the person who booked it, and the people invited (a teammate, by either of their addresses), and a call is `not-found` to anyone else.

**A call's end is kept.** The Room remembers when the first person took a seat (`opened_at`) and when the last left (`ended_at`; for a person dropped as gone, the last time they were heard from), so a card can say "Call ended, 12 min". Calls from before the columns have neither and the card says "Call ended".

**The phone comes through `fetch`, checked against the space's application.** The dial pad's own requests (status, the 911 address, the browser phone's login, the approval for a call, the log, the line's settings, a voicemail's sound) reach meet at `/space/phone` through `SpaceMeet.fetch`, which serves that path and the socket's and 404s everything else. `space-routes.ts` verifies the assertion against `SPACE_ACCESS_AUD` and then mounts the **same handlers** the teammates' door runs (`mountPhoneTeammates`), for the same person, so meet's phone rules decide everything and a second copy of them does not exist. The routes are mounted **only in the app `SpaceMeet.fetch` runs** (`createApp(..., { space: true })`), never in the Worker's own `fetch`, and the path is not in `run_worker_first`, so meet's own host answers a request to it with the page. The Phone conversation is a summary in `conversations` (`phone`) and its items by `phoneConversation`; it is never among the direct messages and channels, and cannot be written to.

**`/start` sends people to the space behind a switch that ships off.** `apps.meet.sendPeopleToSpace` in the declaration (the inbox's has the same shape). `run_worker_first` gains `/start` and `/start/` so the Worker can choose; off, it serves the page from the assets as before. On, a **top-level navigation** to the bare page is sent to the space's Conversations; a fetch of it is not, a local run is not, and nothing else moves: `/start/api/*`, call links, the call API, `/w`, the socket, the phone's webhook.

## Not here

A meeting cannot be cancelled or moved (book another); a Calls list that shows other people's calls; recording; the phone's Telnyx account (the phone shows its not-set-up state until the install has one); chat's notifications and @mentions (through the space's sender).
