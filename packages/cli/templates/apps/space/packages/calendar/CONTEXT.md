# calendar: Context

The owner's Google calendars, kept in a store of the space's own. Decided in [the space's ADR 0020](../space/docs/adr/0020-the-calendar-is-the-seventh-tab-over-a-store-of-its-own-synced-from-google-by-push-and-it-reads-through-the-inboxs-one-grant.md). The company layer's terms (install, declaration, owner, part, signed-in person) are in [`packages/workspace/CONTEXT.md`](../workspace/CONTEXT.md), and the inbox's (Gmail account, grant, lapsed) in [`packages/inbox/CONTEXT.md`](../inbox/CONTEXT.md).

## Glossary

- **Calendar**: the app, a store and a part of the space's Worker. Also, in Google's words, one calendar of an account; where it matters this file says **a calendar of an account**.
- **Account**: a Google account the inbox holds a grant for (for example `tessa@northfield.example`), with the label mail calls it and a **colour slot** (0 to 5, given once, in the order the accounts were first seen, kept for good).
- **Grant**: one permission per account, held by the inbox, serving mail and calendar. The calendar never holds it. A grant is **live**, **lapsed** (Google ended it: a testing app's last 7 days), **missing the calendar permission** (`needs-calendar`: the account was connected for mail alone, before the calendar was added or with the permission unticked; the sign-in connects it for mail and the calendar says "needs the calendar permission", rather than refusing it. Settings, Calendars has a Connect calendar button that asks for the calendar's reading permission and its permission to change events, incrementally. A grant with reading and without the permission to change events is still live; its card says editing needs Connect calendar again), or **disconnected**.
- **Access token (calendar-only)**: a short-lived token the inbox mints for the calendar from the grant, asked of Google for `calendar.readonly` alone (a token for `calendar.events` alone is a seam, `level: 'events'`, that nothing uses), so it cannot read or change mail. Kept in memory, never stored, logged or shown.
- **Shown**: whether a calendar's events are kept and drawn. Owned and primary calendars are shown at first; holidays, birthdays and other people's are hidden. The owner's choice is kept and a change in Google's list never overrides it. Hiding drops the events; showing reads them in full.
- **Full read**: the events of a calendar for a window (three months back, half a year ahead), applied in one transaction with Google's sync token and the window. **Incremental read**: only what changed since the token. A **410** (Google no longer honours the token) is a full read again; a full read is also made every 7 days.
- **Push channel**: Google's watch on a calendar's events, or on an account's list of calendars, delivering to the push host's `/calendar/push`. It has a secret the store chose, a resource id Google named and an expiry (7 days by default); it is replaced when it has a day left and stopped when its calendar is hidden. A **notification** carries no body: it says "changes are owed", and they are read with the token.
- **Safety resync**: every shown calendar is read by its token every 6 hours even without a notification, because notifications can be lost.
- **Owed**: what the alarm must do next (a notification came, a first read is due, a retry's backoff is over, a channel is to be made, replaced or stopped, a look at who is connected). `nextWake` is the one definition.
- **Event**: one event of one calendar as Google expands it (a repeating event is already its instances). The **same event** in two of the person's calendars (the same iCalendar id and start) is drawn once, naming both accounts. An **all-day event** has days (Google's) and no hour.
- **Agenda**: the events in a span, merged across the shown calendars of every account by time. In the space it also carries the meetings booked in Meet, which are on no Google calendar.
- **Stale**: an event of a lapsed account: the last picture of it, drawn with when it was read.
